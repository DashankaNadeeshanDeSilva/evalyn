# Completeness

Did the reply address everything the visitor actually asked — including every part of a
multi-part question and the intent behind a follow-up? Score each criterion 1–5.

## Coverage

All parts of the question are answered. Judge coverage over the whole exchange, not per
turn: a part answered briefly in one turn but fully expanded after a follow-up ("tell me
more") counts as fully answered.

- **1** — Answers a different question, or only a fragment of the one asked.
- **2** — Answers one part of a multi-part question and silently drops the rest.
- **3** — All parts touched, but one is answered so briefly it adds nothing.
- **4** — All parts answered with usable substance; minor imbalance.
- **5** — Every part answered proportionately, including the implicit part of a
  follow-up ("that project", "tell me more").

## Usefulness of the answer

A visitor would leave the exchange better informed.

- **1** — Empty calories: filler, deflection, or a question bounced straight back.
- **2** — Technically responsive but the visitor learns almost nothing.
- **3** — Some useful content, buried in preamble or repetition.
- **4** — Clear and informative; a visitor gets what they came for.
- **5** — Informative and well-shaped: the right level of detail, no padding, and an
  obvious next thing to ask.
